Health Insurance

Your ultimate shield against medical inflation and unexpected health emergencies.

What is Health Insurance?

Health insurance is a type of insurance coverage that pays for medical, surgical, and sometimes dental expenses incurred by the insured. It can either reimburse the insured for expenses incurred from illness or injury, or pay the care provider directly. It is a contract between you and the insurer where you pay a Premium to stay protected.

Benefits & Uses

  • Hospitalization Expenses: Covers room rent, ICU charges, and doctor fees.
  • Cashless Treatment: Get treated at network hospitals without paying out-of-pocket.
  • Pre & Post Hospitalization: Covers pharmacy bills and tests even before you get admitted and after you are discharged.
  • Ambulance Cover: Expenses for transporting the patient to the hospital.
  • Critical Illness Cover: Lump sum amount for life-threatening diseases like Cancer or Stroke.

Advantages

  • Financial peace of mind during emergencies.
  • Access to the best medical facilities without worrying about the bill.
  • Tax benefits under Section 80D (up to ₹25,000 - ₹50,000).
  • Free annual health check-ups.

Disadvantages

  • Premiums increase as you get older.
  • Waiting Periods: Some diseases are covered only after 2-4 years.
  • Exclusions: Lifestyle surgeries (cosmetic) are usually not covered.
  • Complex terms like Co-pay and Deductibles.
