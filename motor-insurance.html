Motor Insurance

Comprehensive financial protection for your cars, bikes, and commercial vehicles.

What is Motor Insurance?

Motor insurance is a legal agreement between a vehicle owner and an insurance provider. It protects you against financial liabilities arising from accidents, theft, or damage to your vehicle. In many countries, holding at least "Third-Party" insurance is a mandatory legal requirement for driving on public roads.

Key Benefits & Uses

Accident Coverage: Covers the cost of repairs for your vehicle following a collision or accident.
Third-Party Liability: Protects you legally and financially if your vehicle causes injury or damage to another person or their property.
Theft Protection: Compensates you for the market value (IDV) of your vehicle if it is stolen.
Natural Calamities: Provides funds for repairs caused by floods, earthquakes, fire, or storms.

Advantages

  • Legal Safety: Avoid heavy traffic fines and license suspension.
  • No Claim Bonus (NCB): Earn significant discounts on premiums for every accident-free year.
  • Cashless Repairs: Access a wide network of partner garages for hassle-free service.

Disadvantages

  • Depreciation: Standard policies don't cover the full cost of replaced parts due to wear and tear.
  • Exclusions: Driving under the influence or without a license voids all claims.
  • Premium Costs: High-end cars or young drivers often face very expensive premiums.
