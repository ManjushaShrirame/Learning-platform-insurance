Travel Insurance

Your safety net for global adventures and business trips.

What is Travel Insurance?

Travel insurance is a type of insurance that covers the costs and losses associated with traveling. It is an essential protection for those traveling domestically or abroad. It acts as a financial cushion against unforeseen events such as trip cancellations, medical emergencies, lost luggage, and flight delays.

Key Benefits & Uses

Medical Emergencies: Covers sudden illness or injury while abroad, which can otherwise cost thousands of dollars in foreign hospitals.
Trip Cancellation: Reimburses non-refundable trip costs if you have to cancel due to a covered reason (e.g., family emergency or illness).
Baggage Loss/Delay: Provides funds to buy essentials if your bags are delayed and compensates you if they are permanently lost.
Flight Delays: Covers additional expenses for meals and accommodation if your flight is significantly delayed.

Advantages

  • Global Support: 24/7 assistance for emergencies anywhere in the world.
  • Mandatory Requirement: Many countries (Schengen Area) require it for visa approval.
  • Legal Liability: Covers you if you accidentally injure someone or damage property while traveling.

Disadvantages

  • Pre-existing Conditions: Often does not cover illnesses you already had before buying the policy.
  • Exclusions: Adventure sports (skydiving, bungee jumping) often require a separate, extra-cost rider.
  • Documentation: Requires heavy paperwork (police reports for theft, medical reports for illness) to file a claim.
