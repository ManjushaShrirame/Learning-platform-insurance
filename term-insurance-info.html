Term Insurance

The purest form of life protection for your family's future.

What is Term Insurance?

Term insurance is a straightforward life insurance product that provides a Sum Assured (death benefit) to your family if you pass away during a specific time frame or "term." It is designed to replace your income and clear any outstanding debts.

Unlike other plans, Term Insurance is Pure Protection. This means the entire premium goes toward life cover, allowing for significantly higher coverage amounts at a very low cost compared to investment-linked insurance.

Core Benefits

High Coverage: Get 100x the annual premium as life cover to ensure your family's lifestyle.
Tax Savings: Premiums are tax-deductible under Section 80C (up to ₹1.5 Lakhs).
Peace of Mind: Ensures home loans and education are paid even in your absence.

Advantages

  • Affordability: A 25-year-old can often get ₹1 Crore cover for less than ₹1000/month.
  • Fixed Premiums: Once you buy, your premium stays the same throughout the policy term.
  • Rider Options: Add extra cover for Critical Illness or Accidental Disability.

Key Considerations

  • No Maturity Benefit: If you survive the term, no money is returned (unless you buy a TROP plan).
  • Early Entry: Premiums increase significantly as you age or develop health issues.
  • Lapse Risk: If you stop paying premiums, the policy terminates with zero value.
