Mutual Funds

Pool your resources and let experts grow your wealth through diversified investments.

What is a Mutual Fund?

A Mutual Fund is a financial vehicle that pools money from many investors to purchase securities like stocks, bonds, and short-term debt. The combined holdings of the fund are known as its Portfolio. Each investor owns shares, which represent a portion of these holdings, managed by a professional Fund Manager.

Key Concepts & Features

  • NAV (Net Asset Value): The market value of a single unit of the mutual fund scheme.
  • SIP (Systematic Investment Plan): Investing a fixed amount regularly (monthly/quarterly) to benefit from compounding.
  • Diversification: Spreading your money across different sectors to reduce risk.
  • Expense Ratio: The annual fee charged by the fund house to manage your money.
  • ELSS (Tax Saving): Equity Linked Savings Scheme that offers tax deductions under Section 80C.

Advantages

  • Professional management by expert fund managers.
  • Diversification even with small investment amounts.
  • Highly liquid - can be redeemed on any business day.
  • Well-regulated by SEBI, ensuring transparency.

Risks

  • Market Risk: Returns are not guaranteed and fluctuate with the market.
  • Management Fees: Costs involved even if the fund performs poorly.
  • Exit Loads: Charges applied if you withdraw money too early.
  • No control over individual stock selection in the portfolio.
